# Roster — the contestants of SMASH YOUR BROS LIVE!

Sixteen champions beamed off their home worlds by the galaxy's loudest game show. Twelve fill the classic
platform-fighter roles; four (marked ★) do things no platform fighter has done. All original designs.

Tone: sincere, over-the-top **bromance**. Everyone hypes, compliments and flirts with their bros, then smashes
them off the stage. "Bro" is a state of mind, not a gender. PG, affectionate, nothing gross, nobody is the butt
of the joke for who they are.

Weights are Smash-style (100 = middleweight). **Open** = playable from the start; the rest unlock through Tasks
(`src/data/tasks.ts`), then a NEW CHALLENGER fight.

## 1. Kit Cadet — "Co-Pilot of Your Heart"
Space cadet from the Comet Colonies: bubble helmet, orange flight suit, jet boots, ray blaster. Middleweight,
good at everything, great at believing in you.
- **Neutral B Ray Blaster:** a quick plasma bolt; hold to charge a bigger one.
- **Side B Comet Dash:** rocket dash forward, hits on the way.
- **Up B Jet Boost:** multi-hit rising jet, ends helpless.
- **Down B Gravity Stomp:** ground: shockwave both sides; air: plunges, spikes.
- **Ultimate Supernova:** a comet slams the stage centre and explodes outward.
- Bro card: *Love language:* matching patches. *Gym split:* zero-G cardio. *Line:* "Houston, we have a
  bro-blem: you're too handsome to KO. Doing it anyway."
- Taunt: salutes, a tiny flag that says MY BRO. Win: "Mission accomplished, co-pilot. Same time next orbit?"

## 2. Sir Clank — "Knight in Shining Bromance"
A hollow suit of armour from the Rustwood Kingdom, animated by a very polite ghost named Lance. Longsword,
kite shield. Disjointed range, honourable to a fault.
- **NB Ghostfire:** a little arcing ghost flame.
- **SB Shield Bash:** lunge with the shield; strong vs shields.
- **UB Spiral Ascent:** rising sword spin, ends helpless.
- **DB Riposte:** counter: strikes back harder than it was hit.
- **Ultimate Haunted Armory:** a rain of ghostly swords across the stage.
- Bro card: *Love language:* polishing your armour. *Line:* "M'bro. I would duel a dragon for thee. Then hug
  thee. Then duel thee." Win: "Huzzah! Thy defeat was most valiant, sweet bro."

## 3. Granny Grapple — "Bro-ma"
Retired wrestling legend from the Bingo Hall Dimension: luchador mask, cardigan, rolling pin. Heavy grappler.
- **NB Rolling Pin Toss:** arcing pin.
- **SB Bear Hug:** command grab into a huge suplex.
- **UB Shawl Lariat:** spinning rising lariat, ends helpless.
- **DB Bingo!:** air: body-slam dive; ground: stomp shockwave.
- **Ultimate Tea Time Takedown:** grabs the nearest bro, pours tea, top-rope elbow drop.
- Bro card: *Love language:* hugs that end in suplexes. *Line:* "Come here, sweetie bro. Granny's got a hug for
  you. It's a piledriver." Win: "Eat something, you're all knees and percent."

## 4. Zippy — "Special Delivery"
Courier squirrel from Thunderwood with a lightning tail. Light, fast-falling rushdown.
- **NB Acorn Flick:** fast little acorn.
- **SB Bolt Dash:** electric dash through opponents.
- **UB Tail Spring:** spinning spring jump, ends helpless.
- **DB Static Charge:** stores charge; the next hit is electric and stronger.
- **Ultimate Thunder Rush:** zips between every opponent on screen.
- Bro card: *Love language:* delivering one (1) hug, signature required. *Line:* "I ran here at 300 km/h just
  to tell you you look great. Okay bye." Win: "Delivered!"

## 5. Professor Hoot — "Professor of Advanced Bro-mantics"
Owl archmage from the Library Peaks: robe, moon spectacles, spellbook. Floaty zoner.
- **NB Arcane Orb:** charge shot; store the charge and fire it later.
- **SB Homing Quill:** feathers that steer toward the nearest bro.
- **UB Blink:** teleport in any direction.
- **DB Tome Barrier:** reflects projectiles.
- **Ultimate Final Exam:** a giant spell circle beam. Pop quiz.
- Bro card: *Line:* "Whooo's a good bro? Statistically, you." Win: "Class dismissed. You get an A in being my
  bro."

## 6. Bouldar — "Rock Solid"
A gentle rock golem from Mount Grumble. Slow, enormous, armour on smash attacks.
- **NB Boulder Roll:** a big boulder that rolls along the ground.
- **SB Avalanche Charge:** armoured shoulder charge.
- **UB Geyser Leap:** launches up on a stone pillar.
- **DB Quake Slam:** stomp; shockwave on both sides.
- **Ultimate Landslide:** the stage shakes and boulders rain.
- Bro card: *Love language:* fist bumps (they register on the Richter scale). *Line:* "Bouldar think you very
  pebble. Bouldar mean that as highest compliment." Win: "Bro… rock… solid."

## 7. Vesper — "Too Cool (Secretly Writes Poems About You)"
Shadow cat ninja from the Moonlit Rooftops. Light, sneaky, combo-heavy.
- **NB Kunai Fan:** three kunai in a spread.
- **SB Shadow Step:** dashes through and appears behind you.
- **UB Smoke Burst:** vanishes upward in smoke.
- **DB Shade Counter:** counter that teleports behind the attacker.
- **Ultimate Eclipse:** the lights go out; a flurry of slashes.
- Bro card: *Line:* "I'm not blushing, it's the moonlight. …You're cool or whatever, bro." Win: "Haiku for you:
  / you fell off the stage / I'll still text you later bro."

## 8. Chef Flambé — "Your Plate Is Never Empty"
A dramatic chef from the Grand Gastronomia with a giant frying pan. Trickster with random dishes.
- **NB Daily Special:** pulls a random dish to throw: tomato, pie, roast turkey, or (rare) the Golden Soufflé.
- **SB Pan Swing:** a big pan swing that reflects projectiles.
- **UB Flambé Jet:** fire-powered rise, ends helpless.
- **DB Hot Sauce:** a sauce puddle that burns whoever steps in it.
- **Ultimate Chef's Kiss:** a giant cauldron bubbles over the stage.
- Bro card: *Love language:* feeding you. *Line:* "You look hungry, bro. Here: a five-course KO." Win:
  "Magnifique! Seconds?"

## 9. Coral — "Totally Tubular Tide-Witch"
Surfer witch from the Sunken Reef with a water whip. Long range, tether recovery.
- **NB Bubble Trap:** a bubble that traps a bro for a moment.
- **SB Tide Lash:** long whip; also grabs ledges.
- **UB Waterspout:** rides a column of water up.
- **DB Undertow:** a whirlpool that pulls bros in.
- **Ultimate Tsunami:** a wave crosses the whole stage.
- Bro card: *Line:* "You make my tides go all wobbly, dude-bro." Win: "Gnarly wipeout, bro. Paddle back out
  anytime."

## 10. Nimbus — "Cries When Bros Hug (It's Rain)"
A small cloud kid from the Sky Isles. Five jumps, floats, very light.
- **NB Raindrop Rapid:** rapid raindrops.
- **SB Gust:** a gust that pushes bros away (no damage).
- **UB Updraft:** floats up on a draft.
- **DB Lightning Rod:** a bolt crashes down from above.
- **Ultimate Perfect Storm:** lightning everywhere.
- Bro card: *Line:* "Don't look at me, I'm misting up. You're just… so cumulonimbus, bro." Win: "Sunshine!
  (I love you, bro.)"

## 11. Amp — "This Song's About My Bro (They All Are)"
Punk rocker from Volume City with an axe-shaped electric guitar. Midrange bruiser.
- **NB Power Chord:** a soundwave projectile.
- **SB Stage Dive:** a lunging dive.
- **UB Feedback Riff:** a rising guitar whirl.
- **DB Amp Drop:** places a speaker that pulses sound until it breaks.
- **Ultimate Encore:** a speaker wall blasts the stage.
- Bro card: *Line:* "One, two, three, four — I LOVE YOU BRO —" Win: "Thank you, goodnight, I'll call you
  tomorrow bro."

## 12. Rumble Ruby — "Pouch Has Room For One Bro"
Champion boxing kangaroo from the Outback Rings. Fast combos and hops.
- **NB Jab Flurry:** rapid punches with a finisher.
- **SB Haymaker Hop:** a hopping lunge punch.
- **UB Tail Pogo:** pogo up on the tail, multi-hit.
- **DB Sweep Kick:** a low tail sweep that trips.
- **Ultimate Title Fight:** rings the bell, then a cinematic flurry.
- Bro card: *Line:* "Applications for my pouch are open, bro. You're at the top of the pile." Win: "Ding ding!
  Good on ya, bro."

## 13. ★ Echo — "I've Lived This Moment 40 Times. You're Cute Every Time."
Chrono-courier from the Clockwork Future. Leaves a ghost trail of where she was two seconds ago.
- **NB Time Bomb:** sets a clock that detonates after a moment.
- **SB Skip:** an intangible time-skip dash.
- **UB Loop:** loops up through time.
- **DB Rewind (signature):** snap back to where your ghost is (two seconds ago) and undo half the damage taken
  since. Long cooldown, shown as a clock over her head.
- **Ultimate Paradox:** freezes time for everyone else for three seconds.
- Bro card: *Line:* "Spoilers: we stay bros forever. Also you fall off at 112%." Win: "Saw that coming. Saw
  you coming. Saw us becoming best bros."

## 14. ★ Inverta — "You Turn My World Upside Down (Literally)"
Gravity acrobat from the Upside-Down Circus. Flips her own gravity and walks on the underside of the stage.
- **NB Gravity Well:** a little black hole that pulls bros in.
- **SB Polarity Toss:** a bubble that flips the gravity of whoever it hits for a few seconds.
- **UB Trapeze Swing:** a swinging rise.
- **DB Flip (signature):** flip her own gravity (falls up; lands on undersides).
- **Ultimate Topsy-Turvy:** flips gravity for everyone else.
- Bro card: *Line:* "Is it hot in here or did I flip the gravity on your heart, bro?" Win: "Ta-da! Bow to
  your bro!"

## 15. ★ Smudge — "Painted 300 Portraits Of Their Bros (Not Weird, Art)"
Street-artist raccoon from Canvas Town. Paints the floor: on their own paint Smudge is faster and hits harder;
bros standing in it are slowed and jump lower. Paint dries after a while.
- **NB Splat Shot:** a paint blob that splats the floor where it lands.
- **SB Brush Dash:** a dash that paints its path.
- **UB Drip Rocket:** a paint jet upward.
- **DB Roller (signature):** a paint roller along the ground.
- **Ultimate Masterpiece:** paints the whole stage and splashes everyone.
- Bro card: *Line:* "Hold still, bro. No— the other side. Your good side. They're all your good side." Win:
  "Signed it. You're a masterpiece, bro."

## 16. ★ Tempo — "Bros Per Minute"
DJ robot from Bassline Boulevard with speaker headphones. A beat pulses under Tempo all match: attacks that
land on the beat are **On Beat** (more damage and knockback). Four on-beat hits in a row: **The Drop**, a few
seconds of extra speed.
- **NB Bass Drop:** a pulse of sound.
- **SB Scratch:** a record that boomerangs.
- **UB Beat Jump:** a rising jump, stronger on the beat.
- **DB Tempo Shift (signature):** cycles the beat slow / medium / fast.
- **Ultimate Drop The Beat:** the stage becomes a dance floor; bros get hit on every beat.
- Bro card: *Line:* "Is this a bop or is it just you, bro?" Win: "That's a wrap. Same playlist tomorrow?"

## The show

- **Max Ratings** — the host: a smug TV for a head (his face is on the screen), gold suit, cue-card hands. He
  has never had a bro. His show turns the bro-energy of best friends fighting into ratings, and the ratings into
  power. Final boss, phase 1: a heavy fighter with armour and boss health.
- **The Big BROadcast** — phase 2: the studio's giant camera rig comes alive (lens eye, crane arms, spotlights).
- **Cue** — Max's nervous cue-card assistant robot, holds the APPLAUSE and OOOH signs. Secretly wants a bro.
- **Stagehand Bots** — the show's crew robots: weak grunts in survival rounds.
