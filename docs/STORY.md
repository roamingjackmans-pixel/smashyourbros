# Story — "Prime-Time Brawl"

**SMASH YOUR BROS LIVE!** is the number-one show in the galaxy. Every season its host, **Max Ratings** (a TV
for a head, a gold suit, and teeth on the screen), beams champions off a dozen worlds onto the Studio Starship
and makes best friends fight each other in front of a live studio audience of nine billion.

Nobody asks why the friendships are the point. The answer: the show runs on **bro-energy**. Every high five,
every "I got you, bro", every tearful post-match hug is harvested by the giant studio camera, *The Big
BROadcast*, and turned into ratings. Ratings make Max stronger. Max has never had a bro. Max would like to have
all of them.

You start as one of four contestants (Kit Cadet, Sir Clank, Granny Grapple or Zippy). Every episode is a
segment of the show: grudge matches, survival rounds against the Stagehand Bots, tag-team bouts, giant
"sponsor's challenge" fights. Win and a beaten rival is "eliminated" — which really means freed, and they join
your corner. Cue, Max's nervous cue-card robot, keeps holding up signs that say APPLAUSE and, increasingly,
HELP.

In the finale the bros work out the trick: fighting each other feeds Max, so they stop. They team up (co-op!)
and go off-script, live, in front of nine billion viewers. Max fights them himself, then plugs into The Big
BROadcast. Pull the plug, and the bro-energy goes home to every world at once.

## Episodes

1. **The Pilot** (Main Stage) — rules, first rival, Stagehand survival. Professor Hoot, the show's "resident
   expert", is chained to the judges' desk. Free him.
2. **Rooftop Rumble** (Neon Rooftops) — the show films "on location". Vesper is stalking the camera crew.
3. **Sea-son of Love** (Galleon Cove) — a "romantic" boat episode. Coral and Chef Flambé (the ship's cook).
4. **Space Bros** (Orbit Station) — Echo arrives from the future with a warning about the finale.
5. **Off-Script** (all stages) — Cue defects and shows the bros the bro-energy meter.
6. **Season Finale: The Big BROadcast** — Max Ratings, then The Big BROadcast.

Each episode: 4–5 stops on a TV-guide map; scenes between fights are short comic dialogue cards. Data in
`src/data/story.ts`. Co-op: the lobby plays the story together; everyone's own progress advances.
